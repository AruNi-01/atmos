## Constraints

- No voiceover.
- Continuous generated WAV from 0.0 to 42.0 seconds.
- No per-scene restart, cue-start stinger, bells, risers, chirps, or transition one-shots.
- App deployable files are copied from project artifacts; `artifacts/` remains the creative source of truth.

## Style Prompt

Atmos should feel like a precise developer control room: calm, premium, and clear. Product screenshots keep their original color. The surrounding brand system should be restrained but not muddy: deep graphite canvas, crisp off-white text, electric blue for system structure, fresh green for running state, and a small amount of amber for emphasis. The video should feel built, not generated.

## Colors

- Canvas: #070a12
- Panel: #0f1724
- Foreground: #f6f3ea
- Secondary text: #c6ced8
- Hairline: #263653
- Blue accent: #6ea8ff
- Green accent: #73d99f
- Amber accent: #e6b86a

## Typography

- Wordmark: Geist Pixel Square, uppercase.
- Interface copy: Geist Mono, medium weight.
- Keep letter spacing at 0.
- No decorative serif, no gradient text, no neon rainbow palette.

## Product Media

- Product screenshots must keep their original colors.
- Do not apply grayscale, desaturation, or black-and-white filters to screenshots.
- Use simple frames and subtle shadows around screenshots so the real UI can lead.

## Agent Media

- Agent logos should appear before agent names where assets exist.
- Show broad support without implying only four agents are supported.
- Use a final "More agents supported" item to communicate extensibility.

## Motion

- Motion should vary by scene: focus pull, directional push, vertical reveal, shallow zoom, and staggered agent/logo cascades.
- Scene transitions should remain clean and professional, but each section should have its own rhythm.
- Text, screenshots, agent cards, and list items should not all enter from the same direction.
- No looping infinite animation.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use the old PNG logo.
- Do not turn screenshots black and white.
- Do not make a noisy colorful logo wall.
- Do not use cyan/purple AI gradients.
- Do not use glowing blobs, fake HUDs, or busy grid effects.
- Do not overload the frame with nested cards.
